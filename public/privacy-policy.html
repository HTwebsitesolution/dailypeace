Privacy Policy

Daily Peace · Last updated: November 2025

Daily Peace ("we," "our," "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our mobile app and website (dailypeace.life).

Status: Free version — no ads, no in-app purchases (this release)

1. Introduction

Daily Peace is an AI-powered spiritual companion that offers Scripture-anchored reflections and gentle conversation.

Currently, Daily Peace is free to use. We do not display advertisements or offer in-app purchases in this version. If we introduce subscriptions or paid features later, we will update this Policy and notify you in advance.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Conversation content: text you type and voice input you speak, used to generate responses.
  • Preferences: modes (Conversational, Biblical, Reflective), saved favorites, and settings.

Voice input is processed to produce a response and is not stored on our servers after processing.

2.2 Automatically Collected

  • Non-identifying technical details (e.g., device type, OS version).
  • Anonymised usage data (e.g., screens viewed) to improve stability and experience.

We do not collect personal identifiers (e.g., name, email) or use device identifiers for advertising or profiling.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain the Service (process your inputs, generate responses).
  • Improve reliability and user experience (anonymised analytics, error diagnostics).
  • Save preferences locally on your device.
  • Send optional notifications if you've enabled them.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 OpenAI

We use OpenAI to process text and voice inputs to generate responses. Inputs are transmitted securely and processed to return results. We do not persist conversation content on our servers after processing. See OpenAI Privacy Policy.

4.2 Hosting & Infrastructure

We use hosting providers (e.g., Netlify) to operate the Service. Requests and responses pass through this infrastructure. See their privacy statements.

4.3 Analytics & Error Reporting

We may use anonymised analytics (e.g., PostHog) and error tooling (e.g., Sentry) to improve performance and stability. Personal identity is not collected.

5. Data Storage & Retention

  • Local storage: Your preferences and saved items are stored on your device.
  • Server processing: Inputs are handled in real time and not stored by us after processing.
  • Retention: Anonymised analytics may be retained for service improvement.

6. Data Security

We use HTTPS/TLS encryption, secure infrastructure, and limited access controls. No system can be 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

7. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access or modify preferences in the app.
  • Delete local data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage.
  • Disable notifications in your device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your residence (e.g., US). By using the Service, you consent to such transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date and post the updated version here. If material changes occur (e.g., adding subscriptions), we will provide notice.

11. Contact Us

Website: dailypeace.life

12. State-Specific Rights (California)

California residents may have rights under CCPA/CPRA, including to know, delete, and non-discrimination. We do not sell personal information.
