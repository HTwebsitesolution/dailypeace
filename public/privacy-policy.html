Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Daily Peace ("we," "our," or "us"). Daily Peace is an AI-powered spiritual guidance application designed to provide comfort, wisdom, and reflection through biblical scripture and compassionate conversation.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, web application, and related services (collectively, the "Service"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Conversation Content: Text messages, voice recordings, and any content you input while using the Service
  • Preferences: Your selected conversation mode (Conversational, Biblical, or Reflective), notification preferences, and other app settings
  • Favorites: Scripture verses or reflections you choose to save

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the Service, including features used, screens viewed, and time spent in the app
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information
  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and access times

2.3 Analytics and Error Reporting

  • We use analytics services to understand app usage patterns and improve user experience
  • Error reporting services help us identify and fix technical issues

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Provide the Service: To process your conversations, generate AI responses, and deliver spiritual guidance
  • Improve the Service: To analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, and enhance user experience
  • Personalization: To remember your preferences and customize your experience
  • Communication: To send you notifications (with your consent) and respond to your inquiries
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate and improve our Service:

4.1 OpenAI

  • Purpose: AI conversation processing and voice transcription
  • Services Used: GPT-4 (for generating responses) and Whisper API (for voice-to-text transcription)
  • Data Shared: Your conversation inputs and voice recordings are sent to OpenAI for processing
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/privacy
  • Data Retention: OpenAI may retain data according to their privacy policy. We do not store voice recordings on our servers - they are processed and immediately discarded after transcription.

4.2 Netlify

  • Purpose: Hosting and backend infrastructure
  • Services Used: Serverless functions, content delivery network (CDN), and hosting
  • Data Shared: API requests and responses pass through Netlify's infrastructure
  • Netlify Privacy Policy: https://www.netlify.com/privacy

4.3 Analytics Services

  • PostHog: Used for app analytics and user behavior tracking
  • Sentry: Used for error tracking and performance monitoring
  • These services collect anonymized usage data to help us improve the Service

5. Data Storage and Retention

5.1 Local Storage

  • Your preferences, favorites, and conversation history are stored locally on your device
  • This data is not transmitted to our servers unless necessary for service functionality

5.2 Server Storage

  • Voice recordings are processed in real-time and immediately deleted after transcription
  • We do not permanently store your personal conversations or voice recordings
  • API requests may be logged temporarily for troubleshooting purposes

5.3 Data Retention

  • Conversation data is processed in real-time and not stored long-term
  • Analytics data is retained in anonymized form for service improvement
  • You can delete locally stored data by uninstalling the app

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Security measures include:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure server infrastructure
  • Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular security assessments

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Access and Control

  • You can access and modify your preferences within the app settings
  • You can delete locally stored data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app
  • You can opt out of notifications through your device settings

7.2 Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request access to your personal data
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data

7.3 Opt-Out Options

  • Disable analytics tracking through app settings (where available)
  • Disable push notifications through device settings
  • Stop using the Service at any time

8. Children's Privacy

Daily Peace is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your country. By using our Service, you consent to the transfer of your information to these countries, including the United States where our service providers operate.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

11. Contact Us
